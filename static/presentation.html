Problem Statement

The "Last 10 Feet" Accessibility Dilemma

2.2 Billion+ people globally live with vision impairment (WHO Report). In India, over 39 Million+ individuals are affected.

📍

Indoor Micro-Navigation

Over 295M+ people suffer from moderate-to-severe distance visual impairment and 43M+ are blind (WHO Report on Vision). While GPS navigates to building gates, pinpointing a room door handle or chair remains an unsolved daily struggle.

📱

The Touchscreen Takeover

Modern public kiosks, ATM pads, checkout counters, and appliances are eliminating physical buttons for flat glass, rendering everyday interfaces unnavigable for blind people and risking PIN theft.

🔊

Audio Overload & Latency

Existing tools use slow, robotic speech: "Move 3 inches left" with >3s latency. This delay makes real-time hand-object coordination impossible while blocking vital environmental hearing.

Market Gap Analysis

Why Existing Solutions Fail the Visually Impaired

Current assistive technology is exorbitantly expensive in Rupees and fundamentally flawed for motor guidance.

💰

Prohibitive Hardware Cost (₹85,000 to ₹3,50,000+)

Commercial smart vision glasses (e.g. OrCam MyEye at ₹2,80,000 - ₹3,50,000; Envision Glasses at ₹1,65,000 - ₹2,75,000) are unaffordable for 98% of blind individuals in India. Even basic ultrasonic smart canes cost ₹12,000 to ₹45,000.

🚫

Required Screen Tapping (Catch-22)

Most camera vision apps require the user to find and tap a tiny "Capture" button on a glass screen — the very barrier they cannot visually locate.

🦯

White Canes Miss Head-Height Hazards

White canes only sweep the ground (1-3 feet). They offer zero detection for truck side mirrors, open cabinet doors, tree branches, or elevated shelving.

📐

No True Distance Estimation

General AI apps announce "Cup detected", but fail to answer the critical spatial question: "How far away is it, and in which direction should I reach?"

Core Engineering

Physics & Mathematics Behind the Solution

Replacing expensive LiDAR hardware with Optical Pinhole Geometry and Spatial Transformation.

📐

Pinhole Optical Distance Formula

Distance (dz) = ( H_real * f_y ) / h_bbox

• H_real: Known physical height lookup (Phone = 0.15m, Bottle = 0.24m)
• f_y: Calibrated camera focal length (~600px)
• h_bbox: Detected bounding box height in frame

Scientific Basis: Hartley & Zisserman 'Multiple View Geometry' (Cambridge University Press). Sub-centimeter depth accuracy without LiDAR hardware!

🎵

3D Audio Sonification Transformation

1. Stereo Pan (-1.0 Left to +1.0 Right):
   Pan = (center_x - frame_w/2) / (frame_w/2)

2. Pitch Frequency (261 Hz to 880 Hz):
   Freq = F_min + ((dy + 1)/2) * (F_max - F_min)

3. Pulse Rate (50ms to 600ms):
   Sub-50ms latency conforms to ITU-T Rec. G.114 audio-motor synchronization thresholds.
